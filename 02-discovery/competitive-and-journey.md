# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses

- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The Discerning Long-Time Viewer: a long-tenured, high-engagement StreamLine subscriber who watches a lot and cares about the quality of what they watch, not the quantity (UXR-01, 02, 04, 10).

- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To find something genuinely worth their evening, quickly, and trust that the choice is good, without leaving StreamLine to check.

- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** They open the app wanting to watch something, scroll for about 20 minutes through 15,000 titles and repetitive "Because you watched" picks, find nothing they trust, and leave without playing anything: for a DVD, a friend's recommendation, or a competitor that hand-picks two films a week (UXR-01, 02, 04, 10 · BUG-1091).

- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** A friend's recommendation (UXR-10), physical DVDs (UXR-01), and a competitor's weekly email with two hand-picked films (UXR-04). StreamLine's own search and "Because you watched" row are tried first but fail (BUG-1080, BUG-1091).

- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:**
  1. Open StreamLine and scroll the home rows and "Because you watched" for up to 20 minutes; the picks repeat the same franchise.
  2. Try describing a mood in search; it only matches exact titles and returns irrelevant results.
  3. Give up on the app and ask a friend, or fall back on a source they trust.
  4. Close StreamLine and watch something elsewhere: a DVD or the competitor's two hand-picked films.
  5. Over time, cancel the subscription because another source does the choosing.

- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most "broken":** About 20 minutes in, still scrolling, when the viewer realizes nothing in 15,000 titles looks worth their evening and closes the app without playing anything. They came ready to watch, and the product had nothing they could trust.

- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD." (UXR-01, 14-year heavy viewer). Supported by UXR-04, a lapsed subscriber who cancelled and now watches both films a competitor hand-picks each week.
