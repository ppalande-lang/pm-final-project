Future-State Journey Map: from 20 minutes of scrolling to a trusted pick

Module 2 · Exercise 2. How Spotlight replaces the Discerning Long-Time Viewer's manual workarounds with one streamlined evening inside StreamLine.

Persona

The Discerning Long-Time Viewer. A long-tenured, high-engagement subscriber who cares about quality over quantity (UXR-01, 02, 04, 10).

Goal

Find something genuinely worth their evening, quickly, and trust the choice without leaving StreamLine to check.

Value proposition

For viewers who want quality over volume, a small curated Spotlight where each pick says why it's there.

Today (the workaround) Scroll ~20 minutes → search fails → ask a friend → close the app for a DVD or a competitor's two hand-picked films → eventually cancel.
→
Future state (Spotlight) Open → see a few curated picks → read why each was chosen → play within minutes → come back next week because the picks were good.
1

Open & Orient

User action

Opens app, sees Tonight's Spotlight first → no scrolling through 15,000 titles.

Internal state

"I've got one evening, don't waste it." Relief replaces choice anxiety.

Pain point addressed

Endless scrolling and choice overload → a short, finite list to choose from.

UXR-01, UXR-12

2

Evaluate & Trust

User action

Reads each pick's one-line curator reason → judges fit without leaving the app.

Internal state

From skeptical to "someone with taste picked this for a reason."

Pain point addressed

Repetitive, untrusted algorithm picks → human-led picks with a visible reason.

UXR-02, UXR-10 · BUG-1091

3

Commit & Watch

User action

Presses play on a pick, or a restored hidden gem → evening spent watching.

Internal state

Confident and settled. No second-guessing, no need to check elsewhere.

Pain point addressed

Leaving for a DVD, a friend's tip or a competitor → watching inside StreamLine.

UXR-01, UXR-04, UXR-10

4

Return & Rely

User action

Returns for the next Spotlight refresh → StreamLine becomes their trusted source of good picks.

Internal state

"StreamLine knows what's good." Loyalty, not habit.

Pain point addressed

Cancelling for a curated competitor → a reason to stay subscribed.

UXR-04

Competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. Choose and watch in one place. No switching to a DVD, a friend's advice or another service; the decision and the play happen inside StreamLine.
  2. Curation that's always there, with a reason. Unlike a friend's occasional tip, the Spotlight is ready every evening and explains why each pick earned its place.
  3. Draws on the whole library. A competitor's two-film email is limited to two titles; Spotlight can restore and surface hidden gems from 15,000 titles the viewer already pays for.

Prerequisites, not part of the journey: stages 3 and 4 only work if playback is reliable on Smart TVs (BUG-1042) and picks saved on one device appear on another (BUG-1058, BUG-1061).

Status: a future-state hypothesis built from 12 interviews and 10 bug reports. Not yet validated with usage data such as sessions that end with nothing played.
